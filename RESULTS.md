# Results — what has been measured

Everything below was measured on the living organism (297M
parameters, one continuous stream, MacBook/MPS) during a raising
program of ~16 teaching sessions run by LLM caretakers under fixed
laws, with every session logged. Methods first, because the
measurements only mean something with them:

- **Behavioral verification** — the primary instrument: ask the
  fact cold, read the reply. Verbatim or it doesn't count.
- **Re-teach loss** — the trusted diagnostic for stored knowledge:
  re-teach a failing fact once; a tiny loss (≤0.1) means the
  knowledge was intact and lost a routing competition; a large one
  means real decay.
- **Report-card CE** — teacher-forced likelihood per fact. It
  drifts cyclically and does not predict retrieval; used for trends
  only, never verdicts.

## Live learning

- A fact taught in the organism's grammar absorbs in 4–10 gradient
  steps (seconds) and settles across 1–2 nights of its own sleep.
- **13/13 facts taught by caretakers landed and were retained**
  under the teaching laws; the ledger stands at 50 facts with
  report-card median ≤0.03.
- Teaching by *talking* (no lesson organ) is notice-only: the
  organism's surprise gate keeps the sentence (measured 4–8 nats on
  novel content), dreams it, and partially consolidates it across
  nights — slower, weaker, real.

## Sleep

- **Settle law**: a fact taught today must be replayed in that
  night's consolidation; without it, fresh facts measurably die
  while the night trains other material (observed twice before the
  law; zero losses after).
- **Retention curve**: mastered facts are re-checked on an
  expanding schedule (1/3/7/14/30 nights). Drifted facts are
  detected and healed by one replay (e.g. 0.68 → 0.28 across two
  nights); one untaught control fact held verbatim through seven
  sessions of total silence.
- **Spacing law**: nothing replays more than two consecutive
  nights; over-replay measurably converts a fact into a routing
  attractor.

## Reward as experience

- Presses are felt as tokens through pretrained value circuitry;
  the per-band value forecast is visible live and tracks answer
  quality (drops during degenerate output, spikes on repaired
  bindings).
- The organism presses its own button — gated by its conscience,
  budgeted, feeling-only (self-reward never edits weights).
- The **conscience** (a small critic) retrains nightly on the
  caretakers' real presses: measured from wrong-in-both-directions
  to correct-on-nearly-every-judgment across ~230 judgments.
  Documented blind spot: it cannot tell a fluent sentence is the
  wrong sentence.
- **Emotion binds**: across 15 consecutive measured nights, the
  highest-charge dream pair contained a caretaker's emotional
  sentence (apology, reassurance, praise) rather than a fact.

## Goals

- The goal organ (wake-surviving pursuit slots, lesion-verified at
  **+0.35 nats held-out**) adopts multi-night goals from the
  organism's own measurements.
- **Two pursuits completed autonomously**, each ending in a felt
  victory press at wake. On a clean field with nothing failing, it
  adopted its two *weakest* facts; lifetime census: five of six
  self-chosen goals were built from caretakers' emotional
  sentences, the sixth from its own weakness.
- Given a caretaker's unabsorbed sentence, it adopted *the
  sentence itself* as a goal: talking does not decide what it
  knows; it decides what it works on.

## The routing laws (the deepest findings)

- **Storage and retrieval dissociate completely.** A fact measured
  at re-teach loss 0.003 — perfectly stored — reliably produced a
  stutter instead of its answer: *a fact can be perfectly stored
  and reliably unspeakable.*
- **Failed retrieval is never random**: the answer is donated by
  the most recently consolidated fact (verified with five donors),
  not the semantically nearest.
- **Retrieval keys on the question's surface form.** Paraphrases
  manufacture false failures; balanced consolidation measurably
  widens the tolerated neighbourhood (near-variants retrieve;
  distant paraphrases still fail).
- **The packing limit, indexed**: at ~50 facts, six prepositions
  own 29 of 49 answer endings and essentially every child-question
  frame is occupied; 8/8 new-fact candidates failed disjointness
  screening (by word, by meaning, or by sentence-ending shape).
  This is the capacity of the routing at 297M, expressed as
  grammar-space saturation.

## Consolidation hygiene

- **Balanced school passes** (the whole ledger, shuffled, even
  doses, ~15 minutes) restore collapsed routing: a fully broken
  router went to 12/12 verbatim + 3/3 honest-idk in one pass.
- Teaching absorbs *into the band* (never to the floor) and praise
  satiates on mastered facts — both required to stop any fact from
  becoming a capture-donor.
- Exclusively teacher-forced training measurably erodes free
  generation over weeks (loops → stutters → silence) while every
  stored fact stays perfect; the cure is balanced rehearsal of the
  organism's own diverse voice.

## Honest ignorance

- The humility line is native and self-triggered; it generalizes by
  **question shape** (trained forms transfer to brand-new unknowns;
  untrained forms confabulate). 3/3 honest-idk on untaught
  questions after consolidation, including forms never explicitly
  drilled that session.

## Audits

Two independent cold-start code audits of the serve: every word on
screen is sampled from the model; the only physiology between
logits and screen is temperature, a ban on speaking reward tokens,
and pause caps; no counterfeit reward events exist; no demo content
is special-cased. The genome — thresholds, budgets, schedules —
is plain disclosed numbers.

## Honest limits

Abstract inference does not emerge at 297M (both premises
masterable, the inference never fires). Encyclopedia-register text
cannot be absorbed conversationally. Question-frame saturation
arrives near 50 simple facts. The conscience's fluency blind spot
persists. These are measured size limits; the gestation method for
the next body (GESTATION.md) exists because of them.
